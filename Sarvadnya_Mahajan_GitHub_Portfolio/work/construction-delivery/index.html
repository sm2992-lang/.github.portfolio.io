The context

The workstream sat within a 25-storey development. It required coordination across team members and vendors, with project planning tools and risk reviews supporting a 10-month delivery plan.

My scope

  • Managed a $2M workstream within the development.
  • Coordinated five team members and 12 vendors.
  • Used Primavera P6, Excel, and risk reviews to support a 10-month delivery plan.

How I approached the work

  • Structure the plan. Used Primavera P6 and Excel to support workstream planning.
  • Coordinate the contributors. Connected the activity of five team members and 12 vendors within the workstream.
  • Review delivery risk. Used risk reviews alongside planning tools to support the delivery plan.

Project scope & output

  • Managed a workstream with a $2M scope within a 25-storey development.
  • Coordinated five team members and 12 vendors.
  • Supported a 10-month delivery plan using Primavera P6, Excel, and risk reviews.
A working principle

A plan becomes useful when people understand their part in moving it forward.

What this demonstrates

Workstream ownership, vendor coordination, project planning, and risk awareness. These are transferable delivery skills alongside my analytics and growth experience.
