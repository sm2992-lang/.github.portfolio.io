The context

The program supported growth across India and six African markets. My role connected cross-functional execution with campaign performance reviews, helping translate customer and channel data into investment decisions.

My scope

  • Led a 14-member cross-functional program supporting growth across these markets.
  • Used campaign and customer performance data to redirect investment toward higher-performing channels.
  • Delivered weekly performance reviews and data-backed recommendations using Google Ads, GA4, and campaign analytics.

How I approached the work

  • Read the performance signals. Used campaign and customer performance data to assess channel performance.
  • Connect analysis to investment. Redirected investment toward higher-performing channels using the performance analysis.
  • Maintain a review rhythm. Brought performance data and recommendations into weekly reviews while leading cross-functional program activity.

Outcomes & scale

  • Improved conversion rates by 45%.
  • Contributed to a 60% increase in African admissions.
  • Coordinated a 14-member cross-functional program across a multi-market growth engagement.
A working principle

The link between an insight and its value is the action a team can take from it.

What this demonstrates

Cross-functional program leadership, performance interpretation, investment prioritization, and a repeatable review cadence across markets.
