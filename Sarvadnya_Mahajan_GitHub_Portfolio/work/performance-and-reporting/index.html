The context

This pharmaceutical client engagement combined digital investment management, performance analysis, and reporting. The focus was on understanding acquisition performance and using it to guide campaign optimization.

My scope

  • Managed up to $1M in digital investment for a pharmaceutical client.
  • Identified funnel inefficiencies using audience, acquisition, and campaign data.
  • Integrated Power BI, GA4, and CRM reporting to support performance analysis.

How I approached the work

  • Examine acquisition performance. Used audience and campaign data to identify inefficiencies in the funnel.
  • Connect reporting sources. Integrated Power BI, GA4, and CRM reporting as part of the engagement.
  • Turn findings into optimization. Applied performance analysis and campaign optimization to improve acquisition efficiency.

Outcomes & scale

  • Reduced cost per lead by 42%.
  • Managed a digital investment scope of up to $1M for a pharmaceutical client.
  • Brought Power BI, GA4, and CRM reporting into the performance workflow.
A working principle

A useful performance review connects a metric with a decision, its cost, and its business context.

What this demonstrates

Funnel analysis, campaign optimization, reporting integration, and investment oversight. The results shown here focus on acquisition cost and the documented scope of responsibility.
