The context

The capstone provided an opportunity to apply customer-data analysis in a structured project. The work combined data organization, cleaning, and visualization to identify customer patterns and generate business insights.

My scope

  • Analyzed and organized more than 10,000 customer records.
  • Used Excel PivotTables and VLOOKUP as part of the analysis.
  • Applied data cleaning and visualization techniques to identify customer patterns and generate business insights.

How I approached the work

  • Organize the records. Used Excel to structure and work with the customer records.
  • Apply analysis techniques. Used PivotTables and VLOOKUP alongside data cleaning and visualization techniques.
  • Interpret customer patterns. Examined patterns in the data to generate business insights.

Project scope & output

  • Completed analysis and organization of more than 10,000 customer records.
  • Applied Excel-based analysis, data cleaning, and visualization in a capstone setting.
A working principle

The value of an analysis depends on how clearly its findings can inform a business question.

What this demonstrates

Applied Excel analysis, data organization, and interpretation of customer patterns through a capstone learning project.
