Chapter 04

The working method

clarity before complexity

A dashboard is useful when it helps someone decide what to do next.

This is the delivery framework I bring to a new problem: define the decision, align the people, make progress visible, and review the outcome.

Start with the decision.

Define the business question, the people using the answer, and the outcome that matters. Give the metric a clear definition before treating it as a target.

A useful starting pointA shared problem statement, success measures, and scope.

Make ownership explicit.

Agree on responsibilities, priorities, timelines, and dependencies. Give business stakeholders and delivery teams a common picture of the work.

A useful starting pointA delivery plan with named owners and decision points.

Keep progress visible.

Use a reporting rhythm that surfaces progress, risks, and the decisions needed. Keep the discussion connected to outcomes and next actions.

A useful starting pointA concise performance review and prioritized action list.

Close the learning loop.

Compare results with the starting point. Separate an observed improvement from an assumption, explain the measurement, and use the learning to guide the next decision.

A useful starting pointAn outcome review with context, lessons, and next steps.
